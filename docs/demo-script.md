# SoundMap: 90-second demo checklist

Record on a phone with sound on. Start from a fresh install, or use **Passport → Settings → Reset demo**.

The demo passport starts at **90 XP** with **4 stamps** (New Orleans, Ireland, Buenos Aires, Lisbon), a 3-day streak, one quiz done and the West Africa pack loaded. That makes the numbers below land in order:

| After step | XP (about) | Stamps | What unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Start | 90 | 4 | — |
| 2. Rio round | ~185 | 5 | — |
| 3. Kora lesson | ~265 | 6 | — |
| 4. Quiz | 300+ | 6 | Level 2 + Caribbean pack |

The quiz gives 15 XP per correct answer (+20 for all six), so 3 correct answers is enough to cross 300.

---

- [ ] **0:00 Hook.** Tap play. The kora groove plays and the rings pulse on the beat. The three chips appear: tap **Africa?**. The plane flies to Mali. Read the harder question, tap **Continue**.
- [ ] **0:08 Name.** Type a first name, tap **Open my passport**. The cover flips to the name page. Tap **Start listening**.
- [ ] **0:12 Play, round 1 (Rio samba).** Tap play on the dial and listen for a few seconds. Tap Brazil near Rio to drop a pin. Tap **Lock in**: the plane flies, the distance counts up, about **+95 XP**. Tap **Unlock the story** and tap through the 3 cards. Tap **Stamp my passport**: the stamp thunks down.
- [ ] **0:30 Lessons → Africa → Kora and the Griots → Start.**
  - Listen First: play the dial.
  - The Instrument: tap **Hear the kora**.
  - How It Is Built: switch on Bass, then Melody, then Improvised runs. Each comes in on the next bar.
  - Swipe to Connections and tap **Finish and stamp**: +80 XP. Tap **Back to the Lab**; the new stamp glows in the strip.
- [ ] **0:48 Learn → Quizzes → Kora and the Griots.** Answer through, including the **audio** question (play the clips, pick one) and the **map** question (tap Africa). Results: XP crosses 300 → **Level 2** and **Caribbean pack unlocked** drops in. Tap **Open SoundDeck**.
- [ ] **0:58 Family tree.** Lessons → **Musical family tree** → Map view. Tap the line from Mali to New Orleans: the "Why" sheet opens.
- [ ] **1:08 SoundDeck.** The Caribbean pack is loaded. Scroll to **Rhythm Coach → Son clave → Play along**. Count in, hit 3 Perfects, see the score. Close, set the tempo to **100 BPM**, tap **Rec**, and tap pads to record a groove. The vinyl spins.
- [ ] **1:22 Passport.** Tap the passport pill in the header: the name on the cover, **6 of 19** stamps. End.

Every step runs offline, with no network.

## Before recording

- [ ] Phone volume up. On iPhone the silent switch doesn't matter (the app plays in silent mode).
- [ ] Do Not Disturb on, so no notifications land in the video.
- [ ] Do one dry run, then **Reset demo**.
- [ ] Record one take in each theme if there's time (Passport → Settings → Theme).
